sq_app: sqterm's code

Everything here is sqterm's; the generator does not write here again. No layouts and no names.json: one screen, written in C++, holding one custom widget, and the addresses written by hand (src/sqterm.hpp).

src/sqterm.hpp         what the files share: the addresses, Shell, Glyphs, TerminalView
src/main.cpp           squared_main: the model, the widget, the screen
src/shell.cpp          the model: the shell in a pty, on the model's thread
src/terminal_view.cpp  the widget: the screen drawn, keys sent
src/glyphs.cpp         the fonts, mapped from the APK; the glyph atlas and its texture
src/prefix.cpp         the prefix: the APK's packages copied out, unpacked (tar + zstd)
third_party/zstd/      zstd's decoder (one C file) and its headers

How it fits together

 UI thread                         Telegraph                 model thread
 TerminalView  -- do term.input  (keys, typed text) -->     Shell -> Pty -> sh
               -- do term.size   (columns, rows)    -->       |  (watched: wakes
               -- do term.scroll (lines back)       -->       |   when sh writes)
               <-- snapshot term.screen (the whole screen) -- Terminal + ScreenPublisher
               <-- state term.completed (the shell ended) --
               -- do term.copy   (two places)       -->       Terminal::copy_text
               <-- snapshot term.copied (the text, for the clipboard) --
  • Shell (the model) watches the pty's descriptor (Telegraph::watch): it sleeps until the shell writes, a key comes, or Android's lifecycle reaches it. What the shell writes is fed to a sq::term::Terminal, and the screen is published whole (sq::term::ScreenPublisher). When the shell exits, the terminal says so as Termux does (`[Process completed (code N) - press Enter], or (signal N)`), sets the state term.completed, and drops what is typed after.
  • TerminalView (a custom node, Stage::custom) reads the newest screen each frame, puts the glyphs it shows into the atlas, and paints: backgrounds, then glyphs, then underlines. Named keys go through sq::term::key_bytes, Ctrl and Alt with a character through key_text_bytes, typed text as it is. A vertical drag scrolls back. Once term.completed is true, Enter calls quit_app() (the App kept by squared_main): the model stops, Android closes the activity, and the next launch starts a new shell.
  • Selecting (12.4, in TerminalView): a long press (0.45 s, within 10 units) selects the word under the finger, and the finger still down moves its end; on blank space it shows the bar with Paste alone. The ends are sq::term::TextPoints, the terminal's line numbers (the snapshot's top_line plus the row), so they stay on their text as more scrolls in. Two handles hang below the ends; a finger within 40 units takes one, and held at the top or bottom edge it scrolls a line each 60 ms. The bar (Copy, Paste) sits above the selection's first line, or below it at the top. Copy sends do term.copy ("from_line from_column to_line to_column"); the Shell answers with Terminal::copy_text in the snapshot term.copied, and the widget gives it to stage.clipboard(). Paste reads the clipboard whole, makes it sq::term::paste_text, and sends it as term.input in pieces of 1 KB as the rings take them, a frame at a time (the paste's buffer, about twice its size, is freed when it is sent). A tap elsewhere, or a key, puts the selection away.
  • Pinch zoom (12.6b, in TerminalView): squared's Stage offers a two-finger pinch to the widget under it before it zooms the UI, and the terminal takes it (TerminalView::pinch): the font's size, in percent of 20 units, is the size at the pinch's start times its scale, to a step of 5 from 50 to 300. Each step makes the glyphs again at the new pixel size (the atlas cleared, the texture uploaded whole) and sends the new grid as term.size; between steps nothing happens. The size is the process's (a rotation makes the widget again), read from the app's files (terminal.font, the percent and a newline) at the first fit and written as the fingers lift.
  • The prefix (12.5, prefix.cpp): squared_main makes files/home and files/usr/tmp, and calls stage_packages: the APK's assets/packages/index against usr/var/lib/sqterm/packages, the one last installed. When they differ, each archive is copied out of the APK (mapped: they are stored uncompressed) into usr/var/cache/sqterm/, so the model's thread reads plain files whatever happens to the activity. The Shell's start unpacks them into files/usr before the shell (the terminal says [sqterm: unpacking zsh 5.9.2] for each), removes the copies, and records the index. The reader takes ustar (files, directories, symbolic links, hard links as copies, the 155-byte prefix field), unlinks before it writes (a running program cannot be written over), and refuses a name or a link with .. or a leading /. Then the shell is the prefix's zsh, as a login shell (-l), or Android's sh when there is none. Its environment: TERM=sqterm, COLORTERM, LANG, HOME, TMPDIR ($PREFIX/tmp, where zsh puts here-documents), PREFIX, PATH ($PREFIX/bin first, then /system/bin), SHELL, SQTERM, and Android's ANDROID_DATA and ANDROID_ROOT.
  • Running in the background (12.6, main.cpp): app.keep_running({.title = "sqterm", .text = "1 session"}) once the Shell runs, with BACKGROUND=1 in the Makefile: squared's foreground service keeps the process (and the shell) alive with sqterm off the screen. Exit in the notification is a quit (the shell ends); a quit, Enter after the shell ended included, stops the service.
  • Glyphs (one for the process): JetBrains Mono NL regular and bold, mapped from the APK (stored uncompressed), Android's fonts for what they lack; a 1024 x 1024 coverage atlas and its alpha texture, which takes only the rectangle that changed. The Stage's surface hook (Stage::on_surface) brings the texture back after Android takes the surface away.

Memory

The model: the Terminal (two 80 x 24 grids to start, then the screen's size, and a 1 MB scrollback), a 16 KB read buffer, the three snapshot buffers (the screen, 12 bytes a cell).

The prefix, while it unpacks (the first start, and after an update with new packages): zstd's decoder (about 100 KB) and its window (1 MB), 64 KB of input and 128 KB of output, freed when it is done; the archives' copies on disk until each is unpacked. Nothing after.

The UI: the atlas's 1 MB image and its 1 MB texture on the GPU (the same at every font size: bigger glyphs fill it sooner), FreeType's faces (about 100 KB each), the fonts' mapped pages (clean, only what is read).
